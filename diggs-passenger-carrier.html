Travel · Crash-tested pick

Diggs Passenger Travel Carrier

Updated September 2026 · 5 min read

Most carriers labeled "car safe" have never actually been through an independent crash test — the label just means it has straps. The Diggs Passenger Travel Carrier is one of a small number of carriers that has been through third-party crash testing by the Center for Pet Safety, a nonprofit that publishes its test protocol and results rather than relying on a brand's own claims.

Quick verdict

The carrier we'd recommend first for anyone who regularly drives with a dog under 18lbs. The five-star independent crash rating is the main reason it makes this list — most competitors at this price don't have third-party test data at all.

Safety rating

The Center for Pet Safety gave this carrier its highest rating — five stars — in independent crash testing, which is a meaningfully different claim than a brand simply calling a product "car safe" without third-party verification.

Sizing and everyday use

It's built for dogs up to 18lbs, which covers most toy and small breeds but rules it out for anything larger. Beyond car use, it doubles as an airline-compliant carrier, and includes a bed insert and a pee-pad attachment system for longer trips.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

Good for
  • Owners who regularly drive or fly with a small dog
  • Anyone prioritizing an independently verified crash rating over price
  • Dogs up to 18lbs
Skip if
  • Your dog is over 18lbs
  • You only need occasional in-home or vet-visit use — a cheaper non-crash-tested carrier may be enough
