Best Pet Supplies Voyager Step-In Air Harness

Updated September 2026 · 5 min read

Step-in harnesses are the easiest style to put on a small dog that doesn't like things pulled over its head, but a lot of them are cut for a medium-dog chest and just scaled down. The Voyager Step-In Air is one of the few built specifically around small-breed proportions, and it's one of the highest-rated small harnesses on Chewy across thousands of customer reviews.

Quick verdict

A well-reviewed, affordable step-in mesh harness for toy and small breeds. Rated strong on adjustability, comfort, control and durability by Chewy's scoring system. Not built for dogs that pull hard — pair it with a front-clip design instead if that's your main issue.

Fit and sizing

The step-in mesh design means the dog walks into the harness rather than having it pulled over the head, which matters for breeds that get anxious about face-first dressing. It's sized specifically for small breeds rather than being the bottom size of a line built for larger dogs.

Comfort and control

The mesh construction is breathable, which is worth prioritizing for small breeds that run warmer relative to their body size. Chewy's review-based scoring rates it strong for comfort and control, with durability as its highest-scoring category.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

Good for
  • Toy and small breeds that need an easy on/off fit
  • Dogs that dislike over-the-head harnesses
  • Owners who wash the harness often (machine washable)
Skip if
  • Your dog pulls hard on the leash — look at a front-clip anti-pull harness instead
  • You need a padded vest for a dog with sensitive skin
